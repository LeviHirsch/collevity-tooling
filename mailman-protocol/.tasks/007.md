~/.claude/settings.json already runs two UserPromptSubmit hooks (lake capture, lake sync) and one SessionStart (entry-router catchup, backgrounded so it cannot inject). Decide whether the scripted check is a third hook or rides an existing one, then settle ordering, added latency on every prompt, and fail-open behaviour.

Decided 2026-08-15: UserPromptSubmit is the receive point - its own script, third in the array after capture_prompt.py and sync_lake.py. Isolated so a mailman fault cannot break lake capture; own timeout and fail-open. SessionStart and Stop carry different jobs (T13, T14), not redundant copies of this check.
